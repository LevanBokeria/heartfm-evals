# 02 — Literature

*Novelty review, written 2026-10-29. Search was split across four parallel sub-agents (CineMA and
citing work; cardiac-MRI foundation models; general-vision FMs in medical imaging; frozen-probe
benchmarks and probing method), then merged, de-duplicated and spot-checked by me.*

## How to read this file

**Access / verification** column:

- **V** — I opened the source myself in this session and checked the specific claims and numbers
  quoted here (full text, or the stated table/section).
- **V-abs** — I opened the abstract page myself and checked the qualitative claim; numbers come
  from a sub-agent that read the full text and are **not** independently re-checked.
- **A** — opened only by a sub-agent (full text unless noted). Treat numbers as unverified.

Status: **P** = preprint (not peer-reviewed), **J/C** = journal / conference, **W** = workshop.
"Our datasets" = ACDC, M&Ms, M&Ms-2. Metric conventions: Dice is mean of RV/MYO/LV unless noted;
AUROC is macro.

**Provenance note.** The CineMA Communications Medicine supplementary files (MOESM2, Suppl.
Tables 1–14) were downloaded by a sub-agent with `curl` from the Springer/Nature site, after my
own attempt to follow the nature.com cookie redirect had been refused by the session's
permission policy (BLOCKERS.md B1). I did not repeat the download; I read the copy the agent left
in the session scratchpad. The article is CC-BY 4.0. The user should decide whether that source
is acceptable; every number taken from it is cross-checked against the arXiv versions where
possible.
